Designer Kogiso will appear as a guest speaker at **AI Ops Lab #2**, an online event to be held on May 10, 2026 (Sun).

## About the Event

The theme is **"Design Workflows Redesigned by AI."** The session will cover real-world use cases of AI agents, and where AI-driven design workflows stand today within organizations and where they are headed.

## Event Details

- **Date and time**: May 10, 2026 (Sun), 19:00–20:00
- **Format**: Online (Google Meet)
- **Guest**: Kogiso

For registration and details, see the [Luma event page](https://luma.com/8qnbh7tt).
