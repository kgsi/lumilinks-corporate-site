---
title: 'Offers and Lumilinks Co-Create a Skill Tree and Book Map for Product Designers'
description: 'Offers and Lumilinks, the company behind CrossRel, have released a skill tree and a book map for designers.'
pubDate: 'June 13, 2025'
---

overflow Inc., which operates Offers, a career platform for engineers, product managers, and designers, and Lumilinks inc., which runs the experimental community CrossRel, have jointly created a skill tree that systematizes learning for product designers, along with a book map for step-by-step study.

For details, see the [PR TIMES press release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000230.000053307.html) and the [related page on Offers](https://offers.jp/worker/wp/skill-tree-for-product-design).
