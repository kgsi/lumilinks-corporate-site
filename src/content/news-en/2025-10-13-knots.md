[CrossRel](https://crossrel.jp/), the event community run by Lumilinks, will hold a new conference, **KNOTS 2026**.

## About the Event

KNOTS, the CrossRel conference, connects the boundaries of design, AI, and engineering to open up co-creation. As web production and software development grow more specialized and divided, the rise of generative AI is bringing design and engineering close together again and giving rise to new forms of collaboration.

The event aims to bring together the people and organizations in the midst of this change and accelerate the development of co-creation, technology, and organizations.

## Event Details

- **Date**: January 25, 2026 (Sun)
- **Venue**: Cybozu Head Office event space (Tokyo Nihonbashi Tower 27F, 2-7-1 Nihonbashi, Chuo-ku, Tokyo)
- **Sessions**: Approximately 30 sessions in total
- **Expected attendance**: Approximately 200 people

## Who Should Attend

- Designers and engineers working in design and engineering
- Product managers and business development leads
- Leaders driving the adoption of generative AI

Start the new year by experiencing a new space for co-creation that broadens your learning and perspective.

For details, see the [KNOTS 2026 official website](https://knots.crossrel.jp/).
