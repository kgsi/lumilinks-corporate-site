Kogiso of Lumilinks contributed to a segment on Watashi to News ("Me and the News"), an ABEMA NEWS program produced by TV Asahi.

Taking up the unnatural AI-generated restaurant ads that went viral on X, Kogiso discussed why they can be identified as AI images, where the hallmarks of AI show up, and how AI could be put to use in advertising.
