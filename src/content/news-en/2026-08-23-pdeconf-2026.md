Designer Kogiso will speak at **Product Engineering Conference 2026**, to be held at Nakano Central Park Conference on September 5, 2026 (Sat).

## About the Session

The session is titled **"Design Harness: Who Ensures the Validity of AI-Generated Design, and How?"**

AI-driven design generation has become a commodity, and the bottleneck is no longer whether something can be built. Yet quality in the field remains inconsistent, and a wide gap persists between "plausible" and "correct."

This session defines the mechanism for ensuring the validity of AI-generated design as a "design harness" and presents how to implement it across four layers: constraints, context, verification, and feedback loops. It shares a way of thinking that lets designers and engineers discuss quality together and decide how to share responsibility.
