Kogiso of Lumilinks contributed to the second feature of the December 2026 issue of **Software Design**, a Japanese-language magazine for software engineers published by Gijutsu-Hyoron. The issue goes on sale on November 18, 2026.

## About the Article

The second feature's Japanese title translates roughly to **"Moving Beyond AI Slop: A UI Primer for Engineers."** It examines why AI-generated UIs feel off and explains how teams without a designer can tidy up their screens by following rules instead of relying on taste.

Kogiso wrote Chapter 3, whose title translates roughly to **"A UI Implementation Workflow in Collaboration with AI."** It covers using a **design harness** to divide the work between machine checks and human judgment.
