Kogiso will speak at **ClaudeCamp | Skill Techniques That Make Your Claude 10x Stronger**, an online event to be held on May 18, 2026 (Mon).

## About the Event

Focusing on the Skills feature of Claude, the session introduces practical ways to use Skills from a non-engineer's perspective, from the core ideas behind using Skills to how to build your own custom skills.

## Event Details

- **Date and time**: May 18, 2026 (Mon), from 20:00
- **Format**: Online
- **Admission**: Free
- **Speaker**: Kogiso

For registration and details, see the [YOUTRUST event page](https://youtrust.jp/lp/claudecamp_skill_online).
