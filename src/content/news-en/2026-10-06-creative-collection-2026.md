Kogiso of Lumilinks will speak at **CREATIVE COLLECTION AI & Craft 2026**, a conference to be held at TODA HALL & CONFERENCE on December 4, 2026 (Fri).

## About the Session

Kogiso will join a discussion titled **"The Unchanging Essence of Designers Whom Others Champion"** from 13:00 to 13:40.

What kind of people keep creating value in the age of AI? Whatever their role, they need something they want to make, along with the range and depth of imagination to pursue it. This session discusses what lies at the root of people whom others champion.

- **Speakers**: Shinichiro Kuwahara (UX Designer, KOEL, Innovation Center, NTT DOCOMO BUSINESS, Inc.) / a Senior Manager from the AI & Technology Modernization BU, ABeam Consulting Ltd. / Kogiso (CEO / Designer, Lumilinks inc.)

## About the Event

CREATIVE COLLECTION is a conference where builders and creators share practical knowledge gained through their work. This edition focuses on AI and craft, and on results and sensibility, with talks and discussions by designers and creators from companies, along with exhibition and networking areas.

## Event Details

- **Date and time**: December 4, 2026 (Fri), 10:00–19:30 (JST)
- **Session time**: 13:00–13:40 (discussion)
- **Venue**: TODA HALL & CONFERENCE
- **Organizer**: Vivivit, Inc.
- **Admission**: 5,000 JPY (tax included)

For tickets and details, see the [official event website](https://www.vivivit.com/events/creative-collection).
