Kogiso of Lumilinks will speak at **Design Node Vol.2: "How Will Designers Survive the Age of AI? The Skills in Demand and the Turning Points in Careers"**, a talk event to be held at Musashino Art University (Ichigaya Campus) on July 24, 2026 (Fri).

## About the Event

With advances in AI, parts of a designer's work are becoming more efficient and automated. At the same time, the areas where people play the leading role, such as understanding the business, framing problems, making decisions, and bringing teams on board, are growing in importance.

This event brings together designers and design leaders working on the front lines to discuss the value of designers in the age of AI, the skills that will be required going forward, and how the role of the designer is changing at both in-house teams and design agencies.
