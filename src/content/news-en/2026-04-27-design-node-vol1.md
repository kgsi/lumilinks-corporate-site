Kogiso, Designer & AI Strategist at Lumilinks, will speak at **Design Node Vol.1: "How Will Design Be Repositioned in the Age of AI? Redefining the Act of Design"**, a talk event to be held at Musashino Art University (Ichigaya Campus) on May 15, 2026 (Fri).

## About the Event

Advances in AI are beginning to transform the landscape of product design and communication design. This talk session brings together designers who are experimenting with AI in their day-to-day practice and unpacks their processes and the reasoning behind their decisions.

## Event Details

- **Date and time**: May 15, 2026 (Fri), doors open 18:30 / talk starts 19:00
- **Venue**: Co-Creation Space Ma, 7F, Musashino Art University Ichigaya Campus (1-4 Ichigaya-Tamachi, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo)
- **Format**: In-person only (no video streaming)
- **Admission**: Free
- **Speakers**: Yasuhisa Hasegawa (Freelance Designer) / Kogiso (Designer & AI Strategist, Lumilinks inc.)

## Schedule

- From 18:30: Doors open
- 19:00–19:10: Opening and event introduction
- 19:10–19:20: Introduction of participating companies
- 19:20–20:00: Main talk session
- 20:00–21:30: Networking reception
