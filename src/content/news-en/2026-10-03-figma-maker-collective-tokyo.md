Kogiso of Lumilinks will speak at **Maker Collective: Tokyo 2026 Oct**, an event hosted by Figma at SHARE GREEN MINAMI AOYAMA on October 20 (Tue) and 21 (Wed), 2026. Kogiso will speak on Day 2, October 21 (Wed).

## About the Session

Kogiso will talk about making things with Figma from a designer's perspective. The session title and time are still being finalized.

## About the Event

Maker Collective is an event hosted by Figma that brings together makers from a wide range of backgrounds. This edition runs over two days under the theme of making things together in an age when anyone can make things on their own.

Day 1, "Figma Update Night," introduces Figma's latest product updates with a focus on live demos. Day 2, "Customer Stories Day," features sessions by creators and companies sharing how they use Figma, across two stages: the indoor Main Stage and the Green Stage on the lawn.

## Event Details (Day 2)

- **Date and time**: October 21, 2026 (Wed), 10:00–19:00 (JST)
- **Venue**: SHARE GREEN MINAMI AOYAMA
- **Organizer**: Figma
- **Format**: In-person (only the 12:00–13:00 session will be streamed online)

For registration and details, see the [official event website](https://fig-events.figma.com/mc-tokyo-2026-oct/).
