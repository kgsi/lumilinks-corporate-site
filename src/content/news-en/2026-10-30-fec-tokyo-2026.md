Kogiso of Lumilinks will speak as a guest speaker at **Frontend Conference Tokyo 2026**, to be held at Sumitomo Fudosan Shibuya Tower (Abema Towers) on December 5, 2026 (Sat).

## About the Session

The session is titled **"The Future of Design Agents."**

Looking back on the changes this year has brought to design in the digital space, Kogiso will talk about how design and code will relate to each other from here on. The session time will be listed in the timetable, which is coming soon.

For details, see the [session page](https://tokyo.fec.or.jp/2026/sessions/kgsi/).

## About the Event

Frontend Conference Tokyo is a tech conference held under the theme of passing frontend on to the next generation. Engineers at the forefront of frontend development share their knowledge and offer growth opportunities to the engineers who will lead the next generation. Under the 2026 theme of talking about the future of frontend, the conference features talks, AMA sessions, and discussions among attendees.

## Event Details

- **Date and time**: December 5, 2026 (Sat), 10:00–18:00 (JST)
- **Venue**: Sumitomo Fudosan Shibuya Tower (Abema Towers)
- **Audience**: All engineers and designers working with frontend and web technologies
- **Organizer**: Frontend Conference Tokyo Executive Committee

For tickets and details, see the [official event website](https://tokyo.fec.or.jp/2026/).
