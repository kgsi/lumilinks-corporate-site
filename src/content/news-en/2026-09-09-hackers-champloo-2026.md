Kogiso of Lumilinks will speak at Hackers Champloo 2026, to be held in Okinawa on October 12, 2026 (Mon).

## About the Session

The session is titled "From Something Built Overnight with AI to a Finished Product, Through a Designer's Eyes."

With AI, it is now possible to build something that works in a single night. What steps does it take to turn that into something people will actually use? Kogiso will share a designer's perspective on the process, using a self-built tool as the example. Attendees will have the chance to try the tool for themselves at the venue.

For event details, see the [Hackers Champloo 2026 official website](https://hackers-champloo.org/2026/).
