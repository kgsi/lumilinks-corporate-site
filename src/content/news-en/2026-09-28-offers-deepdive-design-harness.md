Designer Kogiso will speak at an online event hosted by #Offers_DeepDive, to be held on October 15, 2026 (Thu). The event is titled **"Can We Make Designs That Ship As-Is Without Human Review? Asking Kogiso Where Design Harnesses Stand Today."**

## About the Event

The theme is the **design harness**: an approach that hands AI a template of what goes on in a designer's head, and automates not only implementing designs but also evaluating and improving them.

In the first half, Kogiso will give a lightning talk covering the basic ideas behind design harnesses, practical examples such as Design.md, MCP integrations, and connecting to design systems, and methods for turning a designer's thinking into reusable templates. The second half is a discussion with moderator Tabito Ohtani (CTO, overflow, Inc.) on how to evaluate AI-generated designs and where humans should stay involved.

## Event Details

- **Date and time**: October 15, 2026 (Thu), 19:00–20:00 (JST)
- **Format**: Online
- **Organizer**: #Offers_DeepDive (Offers)
- **Speaker**: Kogiso
