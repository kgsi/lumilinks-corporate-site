On September 18, 2026 (Fri), Kogiso of Lumilinks will give a guest lecture in the **Infographics** course offered at the Hachioji Campus of Tama Art University.

## About the Lecture

The theme is **"The Changes Taking Place in the Age of AI."**

What is beginning to change in the field of UI/UX design right now, and how is AI transforming the work of designers and the way screens are built? Rather than predicting the future, the lecture will focus on the changes and sense of unease being felt in the field, and on questions that still have no answers.

## Details

- **Date**: September 18, 2026 (Fri)
- **Venue**: Tama Art University, Hachioji Campus
- **Course**: Infographics
- **Format**: Guest lecture for enrolled students (not open to the public)
